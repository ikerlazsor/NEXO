Privacy Policy — NEXO

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXO collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the NEXO mobile app. By creating an account or using the service, you accept the processing described here (together with the Terms & conditions).

1 Controller

The data controller is the operator of the NEXO app (“NEXO”). For privacy, legal, and support requests, email nexoapp.community@gmail.com.

2 Data we process

We may process: (a) account and identity: email, Google/Apple Sign-In identifiers, name, username, and profile photo; (b) optional profile details: age, gender, preferences, and information you choose to show or hide; (c) content you create: plans (title, description, date, location, category, cover), plan chat and direct messages, and ratings between participants; (d) safety and social features: friendships, blocks, reports, saved plans, and history; (e) technical session and infrastructure data needed to authenticate you and run the service.

3 How we use it

We use your data to create and manage your account; show profiles and plans; let you join plans, chat, and rate experiences; calculate distances and help pick locations; moderate content and prevent abuse; meet legal obligations; improve the service; and handle support and your privacy requests.

4 Legal bases

We process data based on: contract performance (providing NEXO); consent (e.g. location, camera, photo library, and certain optional data); legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and product improvement, balanced against your rights); and legal obligation where applicable.

5 Who we share with

We do not sell your data. We may share it with: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage); Google and Apple if you sign in with them; geocoding/map services (e.g. Photon/Komoot and Nominatim/OpenStreetMap) when you search or resolve addresses; default avatar generators (e.g. ui-avatars) if you have no photo; and other users according to what you publish. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6 Location

We request location only when needed (e.g. distance to plans or directions). Permission is “while using the app”. We do not continuously track your location in the background. Plan locations are entered or searched by you and stored with the plan.

7 Photos & camera

With your permission, we access the camera or photo library for profile photos, plan covers, or chat images. Those photos are uploaded to our storage (Supabase Storage) and linked to your account, plan, or conversation.

8 Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account in Settings, NEXO deletes the account, profile, associated content, and files you own in NEXO storage. We may retain limited moderation records, anonymized data, or information required by law to prevent abuse or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Deleted plans and files become unavailable.

9 Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures: authenticated access, database controls, and encrypted transmission (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure.

10 Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, and portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on it. You can also delete your account in the app. Email nexoapp.community@gmail.com. If you live in the EU/EEA, you may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

11 Minors

NEXO is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

12 Transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses or other measures recognized under applicable law).

13 On-device data

On your device we may store preferences (such as language), chat read markers, and temporary UI state in local storage. This helps the app work well and is not sent to third parties except as needed to sync the service.

14 Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will reflect changes. When reasonable, we will notify you of material changes in the app.

Contact: nexoapp.community@gmail.com

This document reflects NEXO’s app practices. It is not a substitute for personalized legal advice.
