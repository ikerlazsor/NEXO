Standards against child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA)

Last updated: September 2026 · Zero tolerance

Nexo! is exclusively for people aged 18 or older. These standards explain how we prevent, detect, and respond to child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA; in Spanish, EASI), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any grooming or abusive conduct involving minors.

1 Commitment

We do not tolerate child sexual exploitation or abuse in any form. It is prohibited to create, upload, share, request, store, or link to sexual content involving minors (anyone under 18), or to attempt to contact, groom, or meet minors through Nexo!. Violations result in immediate account removal and, where appropriate, preservation of evidence and referral to competent authorities.

2 18+ only

Nexo! is not intended for minors. To use the app you must confirm that you are 18 or older. Plans are in-person meetups between adults: inviting, including, or attending with minors is not allowed. If we find an under-18 account or use that involves minors, we delete or restrict it as soon as possible and take any further safety steps required.

3 Prohibited conduct and content

Among other things, the following are expressly prohibited:

(a) any sexual image, video, audio, or text that depicts or appears to depict a minor;
(b) soliciting, offering, or exchanging such material;
(c) pretending to be a minor or trying to meet minors;
(d) harassment, sexual pressure, or grooming of people who are or may be minors;
(e) using Nexo! to facilitate child sexual abuse outside the app.

4 Prevention and product controls

We apply product and technical measures to reduce risk: age restriction, visible community rules, in-app report and block tools, review of reports by our moderation team, and the ability to suspend or remove accounts, plans, messages, or other content that breaks these rules or the law. We continue to improve these controls as the service and industry best practices evolve.

5 How to report

If you see content or behaviour that may constitute CSEA/CSAM or any risk to minors:

1. Use Report in the app (profile, plan, or message, as applicable).
2. You can also email hola@appnexo.es with the subject “CSEA / child safety report” and include links, screenshots, or IDs that help us find the content.
3. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services in your country first.

We prioritise these reports. After review, we may remove content, block accounts, and retain records needed for safety and legal compliance.

6 Cooperation with authorities

When required by law or where there are reasonable indications of a crime related to child sexual abuse or exploitation, we cooperate with competent authorities and may retain and provide relevant information in line with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

7 Relationship with other policies

These standards should be read together with our Community guidelines, Terms, and Privacy Policy. Where there is any conflict regarding child safety, protecting minors and complying with the law take priority.

8 Contact

CSEA safety reports: hola@appnexo.es
General support: appnexo.es/soporte

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has created an account or provided data on Nexo!, contact hola@appnexo.es so we can act quickly.
